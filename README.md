# Prison Break

**Prison Break** is a thrilling drama series about *Michael Scofield*, a structural engineer who deliberately gets himself incarcerated to rescue his wrongly convicted brother, Lincoln Burrows, from death row. 

> "Preparation can only take you so far. After that, you gotta take a few leaps of faith."

Using a complex blueprint of Fox River State Penitentiary hidden inside an elaborate full-body tattoo, Michael orchestrates a high-stakes escape alongside a group of volatile inmates:

- **The Mastermind:** Michael Scofield
- **The Target:** Lincoln Burrows
- **The Setting:** Fox River State Penitentiary

Once outside the prison walls, the brothers become fugitives on the run as they uncover a massive government conspiracy tied to [The Company](https://wikipedia.org).
